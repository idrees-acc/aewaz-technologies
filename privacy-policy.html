Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information Aewaz Technologies ("we", "us") collects when you use aewaz.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We are a software and web development company based at 502, Corporate Arena, Sitaram Patkar Rd, Piramal Nagar, Goregaon West, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400104, India.

Information you give us

When you send a message through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you provide it), the subject and your message. If you email or call us directly, we keep the details you share in that conversation.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Tag Manager and the analytics tools it loads to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors come from. These tools may set cookies and collect your IP address, browser and device type. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project.
  • To send quotes, proposals and project updates you ask for.
  • To improve the website and understand which services interest visitors.

We do not sell your personal information or send you marketing email you did not ask for.

Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run the website and handle enquiries: our form-handling provider (which delivers contact form messages to our inbox), our email provider, Google (for analytics) and GitHub (which hosts the website). They process the information on our behalf. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and, if you become a client, for the length of our working relationship and any period required by law. Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent you have given. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to enquiry details to the people who need them to respond. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
