About Aewaz

A Mumbai studio that builds, launches and stays.

Aewaz Technologies is a web and software company in Goregaon West, founded in 2024. Our founder brings 12+ years of engineering on high-traffic, regulated platforms, including security work for a non-banking finance company (NBFC).

Many of our clients are family businesses, a lot of them in the Dawoodi Bohra community our founder comes from. We work in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Marathi.
