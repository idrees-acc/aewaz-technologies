About Aewaz

A Mumbai studio that builds, launches and stays.

Aewaz Technologies is a full-stack engineering studio in Goregaon West, founded in 2024 on more than a decade of hands-on delivery. We ship production software end to end: component-driven frontends in React, Next.js and Angular, Node.js microservices behind documented REST and GraphQL APIs, and AWS or Google Cloud infrastructure with CI/CD from the first commit.

Before starting Aewaz, our founder spent 12+ years in full-stack engineering on high-traffic, regulated platforms: micro-frontend architectures in Nx monorepos, SSR and CSR tuned for search and Core Web Vitals, session management across multi-portal apps, and test-driven delivery with Jest and Jasmine. Most recently, that included leading the remediation of VAPT and red-team findings for a non-banking finance company (NBFC).
