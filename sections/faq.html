Questions

Before you call.

How much does a website cost?
It depends on pages and features. After a short call we send a written quote, so you know the price before any work starts.
How long does it take?
A business website usually takes a few weeks; stores and custom software take longer. The timeline is in the quote.
Can I update the site myself?
Yes. We can set up an editor for text, photos and products, and show you how to use it.
Will it show up on Google?
Technical SEO is built in from the start: fast pages, structured data and a sitemap. Our own builds score 100 on Lighthouse.
What happens after launch?
We stay on for fixes, updates, security patches and monitoring.
Do I talk to a salesperson?
No. You talk directly to the engineers designing and building your project.
